# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary fixes the language for buying a course and for getting the money back.

## People

**Learner**:
A person who buys Courses and watches them.
_Avoid_: User, student, customer

**Instructor**:
The person who owns a Course and decides the Refund Requests made on it.
_Avoid_: Teacher, author, course owner

**Admin**:
A person who runs Cadence and can decide any Refund Request on any Course.
_Avoid_: Team, staff, support

## Purchasing

**Purchase**:
A learner's one-off payment for a single Course, giving them access to it.
_Avoid_: Plan, subscription, order

## Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to return a Purchase and get their money back, with their reason in their own words.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, plan cancellation, reversal, chargeback

**Refund**:
The money sent back to a learner once their Refund Request is approved: always the full Refundable Amount, never part of it.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback, money back

**Requested**:
The status of a Refund Request that is waiting for a decision from the Course's Instructor or an Admin.
_Avoid_: Pending, open

**Approved**:
The status of a Refund Request that an Instructor or Admin accepted, so a Refund is paid and the learner loses access to the Course. Final.

**Declined**:
The status of a Refund Request that an Instructor or Admin turned down, with a reason shown to the learner, so no Refund is paid. Final, though the learner may make a new Refund Request inside the Refund Window.
_Avoid_: Rejected, denied

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase during which a Refund Request can be made, including the whole of the last day.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase qualifies for a Refund: made inside the Refund Window, under 2 hours of video watched, under half the lessons completed, and not a claimed team seat. The Refund Window is judged on the day the Refund Request was made; watching and completion are judged up to the moment of the decision. It guides the decision but does not bind it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, cancellable

**Refundable Amount**:
The amount a Refund would return: the price actually paid when a purchasing-power discount applied, otherwise the course's list price.
_Avoid_: Reversal amount, cancellation amount

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Chargeback" was used for Refund Requests. A chargeback is a dispute a cardholder raises with their bank, which is a different thing. Never use it for a Refund Request.
- "The team" was used for whoever decides Refund Requests. "Team" means a team licence and its seats. The deciders are the Instructor and Admins.
- "Cancel" on its own, as in a form's Cancel button, means "close without saving". It is general UI wording, not a domain term.
