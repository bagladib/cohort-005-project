# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary fixes the language for buying a course and for getting the money back.

## Purchasing

**Purchase**:
A learner's one-off payment for a single Course, giving them access to it.
_Avoid_: Plan, subscription, order

## Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to return a Purchase and get their money back, with their reason in their own words.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, plan cancellation, reversal, chargeback

**Refund**:
The money sent back to a learner once their Refund Request is approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback, money back

**Requested**:
The status of a Refund Request that is waiting for a decision from the team.
_Avoid_: Pending, open

**Approved**:
The status of a Refund Request that the team accepted, so a Refund is paid.

**Declined**:
The status of a Refund Request that the team turned down, so no Refund is paid.
_Avoid_: Rejected, denied

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase during which a Refund Request can be made, including the whole of the last day.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase can currently get a Refund Request, based on the Refund Window, how much of the course the learner has used, and whether it is a claimed team seat.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, cancellable

**Refundable Amount**:
The amount a Refund would return: the price actually paid when a purchasing-power discount applied, otherwise the course's list price.
_Avoid_: Reversal amount, cancellation amount

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Chargeback" was used for Refund Requests. A chargeback is a dispute a cardholder raises with their bank, which is a different thing. Never use it for a Refund Request.
- "Cancel" on its own, as in a form's Cancel button, means "close without saving". It is general UI wording, not a domain term.
