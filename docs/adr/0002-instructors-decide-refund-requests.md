# Instructors decide Refund Requests on their own Courses, with Admins as backstop

The Instructor who owns a Course Approves or Declines its Refund Requests. Any Admin can also decide a Refund Request on any Course. We accepted the conflict of interest (an Instructor loses revenue on every Approval) because the Instructor knows the Course and the learner's progress best, and Admins can step in while the request is still Requested. Decisions are final: an Admin cannot overturn an Instructor's decision once made. A Declined learner may make a new Refund Request inside the Refund Window.

## Considered Options

- **Admins only**: no conflict of interest, but every Refund Request becomes central support work, and the people deciding know the Course least.
- **Instructors only**: no backstop when an Instructor is absent or unfair.
